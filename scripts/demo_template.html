What the comparison establishes

These are one-intervention branch outcomes, not the success rate of a deployed closed-loop selector. Offline best uses the same finite repeats to choose and evaluate; it is an optimistic diagnostic. No model was fitted or selected here. Three repeats do not precisely estimate true success probabilities. Technical pilots and reserved final seeds are excluded.
